# Code of conduct

Project participation must remain professional, evidence-led and respectful.

Participants are expected to:

- address technical claims and design choices rather than personal characteristics;
- provide reproducible evidence for defect reports;
- respect confidentiality, data-protection and licensing boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discriminatory language, intimidation and deliberate disruption;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- avoid uploading sensitive health, identity or security material.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove material that breaches these expectations and may restrict participation where necessary. Concerns may be sent privately to `antonio.clim@csie.ase.ro`.
